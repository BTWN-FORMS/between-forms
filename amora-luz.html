The Philosophy
"Old rooms know something new rooms don't. Amora Luz was made to belong in both."

Inspired by the layered warmth of Mangalore and the restrained elegance of American transitional design, each Amora Luz piece is built to become part of a room's story — not a statement in it.

The Collection

Three pieces. Each named for light — in Sanskrit, in geography, in meaning. Noor, the floor lamp. Vasavi, the sconce. Malabar, the pendant. Made with brass, with wood, with materials that age into something better than they started.

Designed for the interior designer who sources with intention. For the client who wants a room that has always felt this way.

Object No. 02
Vasavi
वसवी — Brilliant One

A wall sconce designed for the moments between rooms — the hallway, the alcove, above a console. Vasavi holds light close to the wall, casting a warm half-moon shadow upward. The shade is pleated by hand, the arm forged in brass, the backplate finished in aged patina. It looks like it has always been there.

Object No. 03
Malabar
The Malabar Coast — Southwestern India

Named for the coastline where the Arabian Sea meets the Western Ghats — warm, ancient, layered. Malabar hangs like a lantern over water. The shade is woven or pleated depending on finish, the canopy hand-finished in solid brass. Designed for dining rooms, entryways, reading nooks. Anywhere a room needs an anchor.
